Privacy Policy

Flicker Flaw by Boulton Games. Effective October 11, 2026.

Flicker Flaw is a puzzle game for Android made by Boulton Games. This page explains what the game does with your information. The short version is that it does not collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. Flicker Flaw does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress on your device: best scores, unlocked packs, and your sound and vibration settings. This data never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

Internet access and permissions

The game works fully offline and does not request internet access. The only permission it uses is vibration, for tap feedback. You can turn vibration off on the main menu.

The Privacy Policy button in the game opens this page in your web browser. That visit is handled by your browser and the website host, not by the game.

Google Play

Flicker Flaw is sold through Google Play. Google handles the purchase and may collect information such as payment details and app diagnostics under its own privacy policy. Boulton Games never sees your payment details.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
